Service 01

Market Research & Consumer Insights

For companies entering a market, launching a product, or trying to understand why customers buy, switch or leave.

Questions we answer

  • How large is the market and who are the buyers?
  • What do customers need, and what do they pay today?
  • How is our brand seen against competitors?
  • Will this product or price point work?

Methods

  • Customer surveys
  • Focus groups and in-depth interviews

Deliverables

  • Findings report with recommendations
  • Market size and segment estimates with stated assumptions
  • Presentation for management or board
  • Clean dataset and codebook on request

Suited to

Corporates, retailers, financial institutions, investors.

Service 02

Socio-Economic & Development Research

For development partners, NGOs and public entities that need reliable data on households, communities and programmes, including baseline surveys and monitoring and evaluation (M&E).

Questions we answer

  • What is the starting position before the programme begins?
  • Which needs are greatest, and where?
  • Did the programme reach its targets, and why or why not?
  • How do communities view a planned project?

Methods

  • Baseline, midline and endline studies
  • Impact evaluations
  • Needs and situational assessments
  • Programme evaluations against a results framework
  • Household and livelihood surveys
  • Stakeholder and community consultations

Deliverables

  • Inception report with method and sampling
  • Indicator values against the results framework
  • Evaluation or assessment report in funder format
  • Anonymised dataset and documentation

Suited to

Development partners, NGOs, ministries, regional councils and public enterprises.

Discuss a baseline or evaluation
Service 03

Feasibility Studies & Project Assessments

For sponsors and lenders who need an independent view on whether a project stands up before capital is committed.

Questions we answer

  • Is there enough demand, at what price?
  • What will it cost to build and run?
  • Does the project cover its costs and service its funding?
  • What are the main risks and how are they mitigated?

Methods

  • Market and demand assessment
  • Technical and operational review with specialists
  • Financial model and sensitivity analysis
  • Risk register and mitigation plan
  • Social and stakeholder assessment

Deliverables

  • Feasibility report in a format lenders and funders expect
  • Financial model with documented assumptions
  • Go, no-go or conditional recommendation

Suited to

Project sponsors, investors, public entities and businesses applying for funding.

Discuss a feasibility study
Service 04

Business Intelligence & Data Analytics

For organisations that hold data but do not yet use it to manage performance.

Questions we answer

  • What do our existing records tell us?
  • Which indicators should management track?
  • How do we report the same numbers every month without rework?

Methods

  • Data cleaning and consolidation
  • Statistical analysis and segmentation
  • Indicator frameworks and KPI design
  • Dashboards and recurring reports
  • Competitor and market monitoring

Deliverables

  • Analysis report or briefing note
  • Dashboard in the client's preferred tool
  • Documented data model and handover

Tools

SPSS, R and Excel.

Suited to

Corporates, public entities and programme teams with routine data.

Ways to engage

Full study or one part of it

Many clients use us for fieldwork on studies designed elsewhere. The scope is set in the proposal.

Full study

Design, fieldwork, analysis and reporting under one contract, with sign-off at inception and draft report.

Data collection only

We run training, piloting, fieldwork and quality checks on your instruments, using KoboToolbox or ODK, and deliver a clean dataset with a field report.

Analysis only

We clean and analyse data you already hold and report the findings, or build the dashboard to track them.

How an engagement runs

From brief to final report

The same stages apply to every service line. Durations depend on scope and are agreed in the proposal. Fieldwork is run by our in-house data collection team, led by the team in Windhoek.

Engagement stages and client sign-off points
Stage What happens What you receive
1. Brief and proposal We clarify the decision, scope, population and budget. Technical and financial proposal
2. Inception Method, sample, instruments and work plan agreed. Inception report for sign-off
3. Fieldwork Training, pilot, data collection and quality checks. Progress updates and field report
4. Analysis Data cleaning, analysis and interpretation. Draft report for comment
5. Reporting Comments addressed, findings presented. Final report, presentation, data on request

Common questions

Before you send a brief

What should a brief include?

The decision the research must inform, the target population, the area to cover, your timeline and an indicative budget if you have one. A short email is enough to start.

Do you work outside Windhoek?

Yes. We run fieldwork in all 14 regions of Namibia, in urban and rural areas, in eight languages: English, Oshiwambo, Afrikaans, Otjiherero, Khoekhoegowab, Rukwangali, Thimbukushu and Silozi.

Which software do you use?

KoboToolbox and ODK for data collection. SPSS, R and Excel for analysis.

Can you handle part of a study only?

Yes. We can run data collection only, analysis only, or the full study. Many clients use us for fieldwork on studies designed elsewhere.

Who owns the data?

Ownership is set in the contract. As a default the client owns the study data, and we keep respondent identities confidential.

How is pricing set?

Pricing follows sample size, geography, method and reporting needs. Each proposal shows the cost by stage. Prices are quoted in N$.

Tell us what you need to know and by when. We reply with a proposed approach, timeline and budget.
