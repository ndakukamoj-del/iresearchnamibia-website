Privacy notice

How JI Research Namibia CC, trading as iResearch Namibia, handles personal information when we carry out research and when you use this website.

Who we are

This notice is issued by JI Research Namibia CC, trading as iResearch Namibia, registration CC/2013/06071. Our office is at 32 Ombika Street, Kleine Kuppe, Windhoek, Namibia. You can reach us at info@iresearchnamibia.com.

We handle personal information in line with Namibian law.

Research studies

When we collect information from respondents in a study:

  • Informed consent is recorded for every respondent before the interview or survey proceeds.
  • Respondent confidentiality is kept throughout the study and after it ends.
  • Do-no-harm principles are applied to every study.
  • Identifiers are handled confidentially.
  • The information is used only for the study it was collected for.

This website

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and collects usage information, such as pages viewed and approximate location. The service is provided by Google.

Your requests

To ask a question about this notice, or to request access to personal information we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it, email info@iresearchnamibia.com.
